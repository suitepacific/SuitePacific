## Blog Post Brief Template

For each new blog post, follow this structure:

1. **Hook paragraph** — State the problem clearly in 2–3 sentences
2. **H2 sections** — 4–7 major sections covering the topic completely
3. **Code examples** (where applicable) — At least 1 real SuiteScript or FreeMarker example
4. **FAQ section** (optional) — 3–5 questions for FAQ schema eligibility
5. **CTA** — Book a consultation, link to relevant service page + 2–3 related blog posts
6. **Target word count:** 1,500–2,500 words
7. **Internal links:** minimum 3 (1 service page, 2 blog posts)

---

## Service Page Expansion Template

When expanding existing service pages:

1. **Keep the existing structure** — add new H2 sections, don't replace what works
2. **Add a "How it works" section** — step-by-step process walkthrough
3. **Add a "Use cases" section** — 3–4 specific scenarios with named outcomes
4. **Add "What to expect" section** — timeline, deliverables, what you get
5. **Expand FAQ to 6–8 questions** (up from current 4)
6. **Add one more internal link** to a relevant blog post
7. **Target word count:** 1,500–2,000 words total
