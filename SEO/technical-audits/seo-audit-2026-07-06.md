# SuitePacific SEO Audit
**Date:** 2026-07-06  
**Auditor:** SEO Strategy Review  
**Scope:** Full site — technical, content, internal linking, conversion, keyword coverage

---

## Executive Summary

SuitePacific has a strong technical foundation and well-written content. The primary gaps are:
1. **Missing high-value commercial pages** (integrations, hire a developer, freelance, custom records, consulting)
2. **Thin service pages** with insufficient word count to compete for competitive head terms
3. **Missing comparison and vs. pages** that capture mid-funnel decision traffic
4. **No integration-specific content** despite integrations being a top commercial intent category
5. **Weak internal linking density** — pages don't interlink enough to pass authority
6. **Missing case study pages** as standalone rankable content
7. **No dedicated contact/consultation page** that can rank for "hire" intent keywords

---

## SECTION 1: Technical SEO

### ✅ What's Working
- Next.js App Router with proper metadata exports on every page
- `alternates.canonical` set on all service pages — no duplicate content risk
- sitemap.ts generates dynamic sitemap
- robots.ts present
- Breadcrumb + FAQ JSON-LD schema on all service pages
- OpenGraph image (`opengraph-image.tsx`) for social sharing
- `force-dynamic` on blog index (no stale caching)

### ⚠️ Issues Found

**1. Sitemap.ts — needs audit** (Priority: HIGH)
- Confirm new pages (hire-netsuite-developer) are auto-included
- Confirm blog post slugs from DB are included
- Add `lastModified` and `changeFrequency` to all entries

**2. Missing page-level schema on homepage** (Priority: HIGH)
- Homepage lacks Organization schema and Service schema
- Should include: Organization, WebSite, Service list

**3. Blog pages lack Article schema** (Priority: HIGH)
- Blog posts render markdown but no Article/BlogPosting JSON-LD
- Article schema improves rich snippet eligibility

**4. No /contact page** (Priority: HIGH)
- Current CTA links to `/#contact` (anchor on homepage)
- A standalone /contact page can rank for "hire NetSuite developer" and "NetSuite consulting" queries
- Also enables proper schema (ContactPage, LocalBusiness)

**5. Core Web Vitals — unknown** (Priority: MEDIUM)
- No performance monitoring data visible in codebase
- Framer Motion animations should be checked for LCP impact
- Recommend Vercel Analytics or Lighthouse CI check

**6. Image optimization** (Priority: MEDIUM)
- Badge images (adminstratorBadge.png, suitecloudDeveloper2badge.png) in /public
- Confirm these are using Next.js `<Image>` component with proper alt text
- Alt text: "Oracle NetSuite SuiteCloud Developer II certification badge", etc.

**7. Missing hreflang** (Priority: LOW)
- Not needed now but should be added if international expansion planned

## SECTION 5: CTA Audit

### Current CTA Pattern
All service pages end with: "Book a Free Consultation" button → /contact  
Most blog posts end with: "Book a consultation" link + 2-3 internal links

### Issues

**1. Blog post CTAs are weak inline text links**
- Blog posts currently use plain text links ("book a consultation") instead of styled CTAs
- A styled button or CTA block at the end of blog posts would meaningfully improve conversion rate
- Priority: HIGH — blogs get organic traffic but have low conversion mechanism

**2. No mid-page CTAs on service pages**
- Service pages only have a CTA at the very bottom
- After the "Script types we build" section, a mid-page CTA makes sense
- "Ready to get started? Book a free consultation" at the midpoint of long pages

**3. /hire-netsuite-developer has an above-fold CTA** ✅
- This is a good pattern that should be replicated on all high-commercial-intent pages

**4. No social proof near CTAs**
- CTAs would convert better with a single trust line: "Oracle Certified | Month-to-Month | No Discovery Calls"
- Consider adding this below each CTA button

**5. Homepage hero CTA is single**
- Consider adding a secondary CTA: "See Our Services" alongside the primary consultation CTA
