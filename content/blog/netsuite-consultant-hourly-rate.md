When a finance or operations team goes looking for NetSuite consulting help, the first question is usually the same: what does it cost per hour?

That is a reasonable starting point. But the hourly rate is not the number that determines what you actually pay for ongoing work. What determines total cost is the cost per completed request, which depends on the hourly rate plus the overhead that gets billed on top of the actual work.

## What do large NetSuite implementation partners charge?

Large Oracle NetSuite partners typically bill ongoing support work at $150 to $300 per hour on a time-and-materials basis. The range reflects specialization: general NetSuite administrators bill toward the lower end; senior SuiteScript developers and integration architects bill toward the upper end.

The stated rate understates total cost for two reasons. First, large firms carry overhead, account managers, project managers, internal coordination, that gets priced into their billing rate. Second, most large firms require a Statement of Work for each engagement, which adds scoping calls, proposal review, and approval cycles before work begins. For a three-hour development request, the overhead can add two to four hours of pre-work billed at the same rate.

The result is that a $175 per hour partner firm can cost $700 to $1,000 for a task that takes three hours of actual development time.

## What do independent NetSuite freelancers charge?

Independent NetSuite consultants typically bill $75 to $175 per hour. The rate reflects experience, specialization, and availability. A generalist NetSuite administrator charges less than a SuiteScript developer with deep integration experience.

Freelancers avoid the firm overhead, so the stated rate is closer to the real cost per hour of productive work. The trade-off is capacity: a solo consultant has limited availability, no coverage during absence, and may not have expertise across every area your account needs.

## What does a boutique retainer firm charge?

Boutique retainer firms structure pricing differently from the two models above. Instead of billing per hour on each request, they charge a fixed monthly fee that covers all ongoing work within a defined scope.

At SuitePacific, Care plans run from $799 per month for 10 hours to $2,499 per month for 35 hours. The effective hourly rate at Care Plus and Care Pro, once the fixed monthly fee is divided by hours covered, is $75 to $80 per hour. But the more important difference is that there is no re-onboarding cost billed on each request. Context is retained across every engagement.

## Why does re-onboarding make time-and-materials cost more per item?

Every time a break-fix consultant begins a new engagement, they spend time understanding the current state of your account before making any changes. They read existing scripts, check what has been deployed, confirm what record types are in use, and identify dependencies. That investigation time is billed at the full hourly rate.

For a three-hour development task, re-onboarding typically adds one to three hours. Over a year of monthly requests, the accumulated re-onboarding cost often exceeds the full annual cost of a retainer at a comparable effective rate.

The break-fix model is genuinely cheaper when your account needs support only once or twice a year for isolated, straightforward items. It becomes more expensive than a retainer when your account generates regular ongoing work.

## How should you compare NetSuite consultant cost across models?

The most useful comparison is cost per completed item rather than hourly rate. To estimate this:

**Time-and-materials:** Estimated productive hours for the task plus estimated re-onboarding hours, multiplied by the stated hourly rate. Add any minimum engagement requirements.

**Retainer:** Monthly fee divided by number of requests completed in a typical month. This is the true cost per item under a retainer, and it falls as request volume increases because the fixed fee is spread across more work.

For most accounts with three or more ongoing requests per month, the retainer model produces a lower cost per completed item than time-and-materials at any comparable rate.

## What affects NetSuite consultant cost the most?

Four factors drive cost beyond the stated hourly rate:

**Billing model.** Time-and-materials adds scoping and approval overhead to every request. Retainers absorb that overhead in the fixed fee.

**Context retention.** Re-onboarding on each engagement adds one to three hours of billed time per request. Retained context eliminates this cost entirely.

**Firm size.** Larger firms carry more overhead, which is priced into their rates regardless of how efficiently the actual work gets done.

**Specialization.** SuiteScript developers and integration architects command higher rates than generalists. The relevant question is whether you need specialized expertise consistently or only occasionally.
