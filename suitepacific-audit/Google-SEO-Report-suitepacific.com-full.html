2. Audit Categories

2.1 Technical SEO

Score: 84/100

What Works

  • Full SSR on all pages — content in initial HTML, no JavaScript rendering dependency
  • Security headers: HSTS preload (730 days), nosniff, Referrer-Policy, Permissions-Policy
  • Sitemap: 94 URLs, all 200 OK, all with accurate lastmod, declared in robots.txt
  • Canonical tags present on all content pages
  • Redirect chains: HTTP to HTTPS in one hop, www to non-www in one hop, no multi-hop chains
  • TTFB: 60-83ms (Vercel CDN HIT on all audited pages)
  • AI crawlers explicitly named in robots.txt with Allow: /
  • llms.txt: present, complete (35/35 blog posts indexed), 15+ FAQ pairs, RSL 1.0 licensed

Findings

og:image missing on all 12 service pages High

Every service page defines its own openGraph metadata block without an images key. Next.js App Router replaces (not merges) the root layout openGraph, stripping the og-default.png fallback. All 12 service pages render text-only social cards. Confirmed missing via source code review and live HTTP header inspection.

Recommendation: Add images: [{ url: 'https://suitepacific.com/og-default.png', width: 1200, height: 630 }] to every service page openGraph block. Consider extracting a shared defaultOpenGraph constant in lib/seo.ts to prevent future recurrence.

/suitecompare marketing page missing canonical and og:image High

The /suitecompare product landing page is correctly indexed (robots: index, follow override in page.tsx) and in the sitemap, but lacks an alternates.canonical self-reference and an og:image.

Recommendation: Add alternates: { canonical: 'https://suitepacific.com/suitecompare' } and openGraph images to app/suitecompare/page.tsx metadata.

No IndexNow implementation Medium

No IndexNow key file found. With 6-12 new posts per month, Bing indexing relies entirely on crawl discovery. IndexNow primarily benefits Bing Copilot citation freshness.

Recommendation: Generate a UUID key, place it at /public/[key].txt, ping api.indexnow.org on each deploy or publish event.

CSP frame-ancestors 'none' conflicts with X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN Medium

Two headers express contradictory framing policies. Modern browsers follow CSP; XFO is redundant and inconsistent.

Recommendation: Align both headers: either frame-ancestors 'none' + X-Frame-Options: DENY, or frame-ancestors 'self' + X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN.

CSP script-src contains unsafe-inline and unsafe-eval Medium

These directives negate XSS protection. Next.js 15 App Router supports nonce-based CSP to eliminate both.

Recommendation: Implement nonce-based CSP via Next.js middleware.

X-XSS-Protection header is deprecated Low

X-XSS-Protection: 1; mode=block is not recognized by modern browsers and can cause regressions on legacy environments.

Recommendation: Set X-XSS-Protection: 0 per OWASP. A properly scoped CSP replaces its function.

Case study sitemap lastmod values all share one date Low

All 6 case studies carry lastmod: 2026-06-01 (a batch date, not individual modification dates). Google uses lastmod for re-crawl scheduling.

Recommendation: Derive sitemap lastmod from each case study's actual publication date field.

What Works

  • H1 tags present on all pages

Findings

Homepage title cannibalizes /netsuite-post-go-live-support High

Homepage title 'SuitePacific: Post-Go-Live NetSuite Support & Optimization' directly targets the same keyword cluster as the dedicated service page. The homepage likely outranks the dedicated page, sending commercial traffic to the wrong destination.

Recommendation: Revise homepage title to a brand-hub statement. Give /netsuite-post-go-live-support full keyword ownership.

Meta descriptions over 160 characters on service pages Medium

Service page meta descriptions range 182-202 characters. Google truncates at ~160 characters, reducing CTR from the truncation point.

Recommendation: Trim service page meta descriptions to 150-160 characters. Front-load the primary benefit.

Blog posts lack H3 sub-headings for granular passage indexing Medium

All posts use only H2 headings. Posts over 1,500 words covering 3+ subtopics per H2 would benefit from H3 sub-sections for passage-level indexing.

Recommendation: Add H3 headings to posts over 1,500 words. Priority: netsuite-workflow-vs-suitescript, netsuite-map-reduce-script-guide, netsuite-saved-search-examples.

Blog post titles over 60 characters (SERP truncation) Low

All checked blog post titles exceed 60 characters (70-92 range) due to '| SuitePacific' suffix. Google truncates at ~60 characters in most SERPs.

Recommendation: Where title + ' | SuitePacific' exceeds 65 characters, shorten the title itself. Low priority — cosmetic, not a ranking factor.

2.4 Schema / Structured Data

Score: 72/100

What Works

  • BlogPosting schema on all 35 blog posts with correct dateModified (uses updated field when available)
  • TechArticle schema on all 30 resource pages
  • Service schema on all 11 service pages with BreadcrumbList
  • ProfessionalService + WebSite + FAQPage on homepage
  • SoftwareApplication on /suitecompare
  • Article schema on all 6 case study pages
  • ItemList schema on case studies index
  • All @context use https://schema.org, all dates are ISO 8601, all URLs are absolute

Findings

Resource TechArticle schema image: logo-icon.png (256x256) declared as 1200x630 Medium

app/(site)/resources/[slug]/page.tsx line 63 sets the TechArticle image to logo-icon.png with width: 1200, height: 630. The actual file is 256x256. Google validates image dimensions for Article rich results; a 256px-wide image fails the 1200px minimum and blocks all 30 resource pages from image-enhanced rich results.

Recommendation: Change image URL to og-default.png (confirmed 1200x630) in app/(site)/resources/[slug]/page.tsx line 63.

Article/TechArticle/BlogPosting missing url property Low

mainEntityOfPage provides the WebPage @id, but article entities should also carry a url property. Affects BlogPostingJsonLd (components/seo/JsonLd.tsx), case study Article, and TechArticle in resources/[slug]/page.tsx.

Recommendation: Add url: absolute-canonical-url to each article block. Three one-line additions.

Publisher logo ImageObject missing width and height Low

The publisher.logo ImageObject in BlogPosting, Article, and TechArticle blocks declares url: logo-icon.png with no width or height. Actual dimensions are 256x256.

Recommendation: Add width: 256, height: 256 to all three publisher logo declarations.

/netsuite-implementation-partner-vs-managed-support missing Article schema Low

This long-form comparison guide has only BreadcrumbList and FAQPage. No Article schema means it is ineligible for Article rich results.

Recommendation: Add an inline Article block using og-default.png as the image.

Blog and Resources index pages missing collection schema Low

/blog has only BreadcrumbList. /resources has only BreadcrumbList.

Recommendation: Add Blog schema to /blog and CollectionPage schema to /resources.

ProfessionalService missing @id anchor Low

OrganizationJsonLd has no @id, so service pages cannot reference the organization entity by ID. Limits Google's entity graph connection.

Recommendation: Add '@id': 'https://suitepacific.com/#organization' to OrganizationJsonLd.

FAQPage no longer produces Google rich results (info only) Info

Google retired FAQ rich results for all sites on May 7, 2026. FAQPage blocks are harmless but produce no SERP feature.

Recommendation: No action needed. Do not remove existing blocks.

2.6 AI Search Readiness (GEO)

Score: 56/100

What Works

  • AI crawlers explicitly allowed: GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot all have named User-Agent blocks with Allow: /
  • Full SSR — content accessible without JavaScript to all AI crawlers
  • llms.txt: present, complete (35/35 blog posts indexed), 15+ FAQ pairs, RSL 1.0 licensed
  • Quick Answer boxes on 12 posts (134-167 words, self-contained)
  • All 35 posts under 3 months old — within the 3x AI citation boost window

Findings

Zero question-based H2 headings across all 35 posts High

All posts use declarative H2s. AI systems match section headings against user queries. Question headings ('When should you use a Client Script?') match directly; declarative headings ('What a Client Script actually is') require the AI to infer intent.

Recommendation: Rephrase H2s on 5 priority posts to question format: netsuite-workflow-vs-suitescript, netsuite-map-reduce-script-guide, netsuite-user-event-vs-client-script, suitescript-best-practices, netsuite-post-go-live-checklist.

Blog post openers too short for AI citation (40-85 words vs 134-167 optimal) High

~44% of AI citations come from the first 30% of a page. Current openers are narrative hooks, not answer blocks. Posts without Quick Answer boxes have no self-contained citable passage near the top.

Recommendation: For the 23 posts without Quick Answer boxes, add a 130-160 word Quick Summary block immediately after the first paragraph.

SVG diagram content not accessible to AI crawlers Medium

Technical posts embed inline SVGs with text elements. AI crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot) do not parse SVG text. Every insight locked inside an SVG is uncitable. Approximately 10-15 diagrams affected.

Recommendation: Add a plain-text figcaption after each SVG diagram stating the key conclusion in 1-2 sentences.

No Reddit or YouTube brand presence Medium

YouTube mentions correlate 0.737 with AI citation rates (Ahrefs 75K-brand study, strongest single off-site signal). Reddit correlates at 0.46+. Both are absent.

Recommendation: Start with r/NetSuite (~45,000 members): answer 2-3 SuiteScript/workflow questions per week using genuine expertise.
